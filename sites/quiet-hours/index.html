Design choices

Twenty switches, one number.

Every row carries its volume

"About 31 a week" sits under the category name, so the biggest source of noise is obvious before anything changes.

Three choices, with a middle

Right away, Morning summary, or Off. Most notifications are worth knowing about and not worth being interrupted for.

One thing you cannot turn off

Security alerts have no control, and the row says why. With the reason, a locked setting reads as protection.

The honest limit

A preview is only as good as the history behind it.

A new account has no history, and a typical week is a poor guide to an unusual one. A real version would need a sensible starting estimate and a way to say "based on very little so far".
