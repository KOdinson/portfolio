How often should your phone interrupt you?

Quiet Hours is a notification settings page that shows what your choices add up to. Same account, three presets, three very different weeks:

40Everything, right away
15Only what needs me
1Nearly silent

Interruptions per week, drawn to scale. Sample volumes for one invented account.

Overnight, everything waits.

With quiet hours on, anything that arrives at night is held for a 7 am summary. Security alerts still come through, and the setting says so.

How it works

Each row has three choices and shows its own volume.

Security alertsAbout 1 a week · always sent, and the row says whyLocked on
Mentions and repliesAbout 14 a weekRight away
Activity on things you followAbout 31 a week · the biggest source of noiseMorning summary
Product news and tipsAbout 3 a weekOff

The middle option is the point. Most notifications are worth knowing about and not worth being interrupted for.

Live prototype with sample volumes. Try the three presets, then change one row and watch the number.

The honest limit: a preview is only as good as the history behind it. A new account would need a starting estimate and a way to say "based on very little so far".
