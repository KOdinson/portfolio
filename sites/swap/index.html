The gap

Between "can you cover me?" and an approved schedule, whose shift is it?

That is when both people can believe the other one is working Friday. Swap never leaves it open.

You have askedFriday is still your shift. Nobody has accepted yet.
Luis says yesFriday is still your shift until a manager approves.
Manager approvesFriday is Luis's shift now. You are off.
How it works

The rules run before the ask.

Nobody likes asking a coworker for a favour and then learning from a manager, a day later, that the coworker was never allowed to say yes.

Swap shows who can take the shift and who cannot, with the reason. Each reason is about the rule, never the person, and is written so it could be read aloud in front of the coworker it names.

LuisFree Friday evening. Would be at 32 hours this week.
Dana can't take itWould reach 44 hours. Overtime needs a manager to offer it.
Tom can't take itWorks until 7 am Friday. That leaves 8 hours of rest, and the minimum is 10.
Three steps, kept separate

Asked, accepted and approved are different things.

  1. Ask several at onceThe first yes gets it. Everyone else is told it is taken.
  2. Someone acceptsThe shift is still yours. The screen says so.
  3. A manager approves"The swap is not real until this step." Then the schedule changes for everyone.
