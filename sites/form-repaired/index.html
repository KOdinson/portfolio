The eight repairs

Each one maps to a WCAG 2.2 success criterion.

  1. A visible label on every field3.3.2 · 1.3.1 · 4.1.2
  2. Text contrast of 6.5 to 1 or better1.4.3
  3. "Optional" as a word, not an asterisk1.4.1
  4. A format example on the date3.3.2
  5. Errors that name the field and the fix3.3.1 · 3.3.3
  6. A visible focus ring2.4.7
  7. Targets at least 44 pixels tall2.5.8
  8. Fields the browser can fill for you1.3.5
Why it matters

Accessible is also just better.

Nobody is helped alone

Labels that stay put help anyone who looks away mid-field. The repaired form is easier on a phone, in sunlight and when tired.

Errors as instructions

"Enter an email address, like name@example.com." The word "invalid" does not appear.

The floor, not the ceiling

Built to the criteria and measured for contrast. Not yet tested with people who use assistive technology, and the page says so.
