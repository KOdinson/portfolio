The floor, not the ceiling

Built to the criteria. Not yet tested with the people it is for.

Five of the eight repairs are words: a label, a hint, an example, an error message, the word "optional". Meeting WCAG is where accessibility starts. Sessions with people who use screen readers and voice control come next, and they regularly find what a checklist misses.
