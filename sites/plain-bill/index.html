Why is it higher this month?

Plain Bill answers the question people really bring to a bill. It states the change from last month, gives the reasons in dollars, and makes them add up.

Try the prototypeHow it works

Live prototype with an invented account. Hover the bars, or switch the chart to a table.

The reasons reconcile

Three causes. One total. No remainder.

A partial explanation invites suspicion. One that adds up exactly closes the question.

Design choices

Written for the person, not the meter.

Dollars, not kilowatt-hours

Every figure is money. The conversion from technical units is done once, by the system.

One bar in colour

Twelve months, with only the current one highlighted. The chart has one point to make.

Which reasons repeat

A long billing period is a one-off. A rate rise is permanent. The page says which is which, then forecasts the next two bills.

The honest limit

An estimate to the dollar can mislead.

Splitting a bill change into exact causes is harder than a prototype makes it look. Weather and behaviour overlap. A real version would need to show ranges and say when it is unsure.
