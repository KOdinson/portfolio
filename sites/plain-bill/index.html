Written for the person, not the meter.

  • Dollars, not kilowatt-hoursEvery figure is money. The conversion from technical units is done once, by the system.
  • "None of them is a mistake on your bill"The page answers the worry directly, then earns the claim with the three reasons.
  • One bar in colourTwelve months, with only the current one highlighted. The chart has one point to make.
  • Which reasons repeatA long billing period is a one-off. A rate rise is permanent. The page says which is which, then forecasts the next two bills.
  • The honest limitSplitting a change into exact causes is an estimate. A real version would show ranges and say when it is unsure.
