What arrives first, and is something handling it?

A redesigned operator display for an autonomous counter-drone swarm. The interceptors decide what to chase. The operator supervises, and stops the system when it is wrong.

01 / THE DISPLAY

Live prototype with simulated tracks. Select a threat and hold its intercept, or press and hold Abort.

02 / THREE QUESTIONS

The screen is organised around a decision, not around the system.

What arrives first?A queue sorted by seconds to the perimeter. The time is the largest number on each row.
Is something handling it?Each row states its pairing as a sentence: W2 intercepting, 6 s to contact
Do I need to step in?One control per threat to hold the intercept. One guarded control to stop everything.

04 / GUARDS

Routine actions are one click. Abort takes a deliberate hold.

Hold to abortA 1.2 second press, by pointer or by key. The button fills as you hold, so the delay explains itself.
Autonomy you can overruleHolding a threat stops any interceptor engaging it. The log reads "You put T3 on hold", so operator and system actions are never confused.
Simulation you cannot missA hazard-striped marker beside the title on every frame, where a live build would carry the opposite marker.
