▲ Partial outage · this is what a headline should do

Some card payments are failing. Everything else is working.

Someone who reads only that sentence knows the scope. This concept rewrites the outage page for the person on the other side of it, and this site is laid out the way that page would be.

What we know: four fixed questions

  1. What is happeningIn terms the customer can see, with the time it began.
  2. Who is affectedAnd, just as useful, who is not. Tick the parts you use and the page says "This affects you: payments" or "This does not affect anything you use".
  3. What you can doIncluding what will not help: "Resetting your password will not help."
  4. When it will be fixedOr an honest "We do not know yet", with a committed time for the next update.

Updates, rewritten

  1. 2:04 pmIdentified — The issue has been identified and a fix is being implemented.The processor has confirmed the fault is on their side and is working on it. We have no time for a fix yet.
  2. 1:51 pmInvestigating — We are currently investigating this issue.We are seeing card payments fail and are finding out why.

Live prototype with an invented service. Switch between the four moments, and untick a part you do not use.

After the fix, the same shape

How long it lasted, a plain statement that the cause was the company's own update, and a date for the full account. The honest limit of all this: a template cannot get accurate information out of a team that is busy fixing the problem. Someone has to own keeping the page true.
