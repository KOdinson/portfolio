Some card payments are failing. Everything else is working.

That sentence is a status page headline. Someone who reads only that line knows the scope. This concept rewrites the outage page for the person on the other side of it.

The same outage, written twice

Status pages are usually written for engineers.

Typical● payments-api — Degraded PerformanceInvestigating — We are currently investigating this issue.
▲ Partial outageSome card payments are failing. Everything else is working.Try the payment again. A second attempt usually goes through.
Four fixed questions

Always these four, always in this order.

What is happeningIn terms the customer can see, with the time it began.
Who is affectedAnd, just as useful, who is not.
What you can doIncluding what will not help: "Resetting your password will not help."
When it will be fixedOr an honest "we do not know yet", with a committed time for the next update.
Does this affect me?

The same outage is urgent for one reader and irrelevant for the next.

A line of your own

Tick the parts you use and the page says "This affects you: payments", or "This does not affect anything you use".

Knock-on effects, named

When sign-in is down, payments read "Unreachable until sign-in returns". One problem, not four.

After the fix, the same shape

How long it lasted, a plain statement of the cause, and a date for the full account.
