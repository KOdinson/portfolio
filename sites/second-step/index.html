Second Step How it worksTry itCase study

A second step that people finish.

Second Step is a two-step sign-in setup that tells the truth about each option, writes errors you can act on, and will not let you finish without a way back in.

Try the prototypeHow it works

Live prototype. Nothing in it is real. Any six digits work as the code.

Honest options

Three choices, with their real weaknesses.

Three equal choices is a quiz. A marked default with honest alternatives is advice.

RecommendedAuthenticator appWorks with no signal, and codes cannot be redirected to someone else.
Text messageEasiest to start. Weaker, because a phone number can be taken over. Still far better than a password alone.
Security keyThe strongest option. You need to buy a small device and keep it with you.
Errors that count for you

Each message says what the system saw.

Error: Codes are 6 digits. You entered 4.
Error: Codes are numbers only. Check for a letter or symbol.
Recovery is part of setup

The day you lose your phone should not be the day you lose your account.

Backup codes cannot be skipped

Finish stays disabled until you confirm the codes are saved somewhere other than this phone.

The warning names the likely mistake

"Do not keep them only on the phone you just set up."

The last screen answers three questions

What changes, what if you lose your phone, and what if you lose the codes too. The third admits a wait of days and explains why.
