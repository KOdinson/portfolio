A second step that people finish.

A two-step sign-in setup that tells the truth about each option, writes errors you can act on, and will not let you finish without a way back in.

Try the prototypeWalk the four steps

Choose, knowing the trade-offs

Authenticator app · RecommendedWorks with no signal, and codes cannot be redirected to someone else.
Text messageEasiest to start. Weaker, because a phone number can be taken over. Still far better than a password alone.
Security keyThe strongest option. You need to buy a small device and keep it with you.

Three equal choices is a quiz. A marked default with honest alternatives is advice.

Connect it

Scan with the app, or type the key if the camera will not cooperate. The button repeats your choice back to you: "Continue with text message".

Prove it worked

Error: Codes are 6 digits. You entered 4.
Error: Codes are numbers only. Check for a letter or symbol.

Each message says what the system saw, which tells you what to fix.

Save your way back in

XXXX-XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX-XXXX

Finish stays disabled until you confirm the backup codes are saved "somewhere other than this phone". The reason sits beside the button: "This is the one step people regret skipping."

Done, with three questions answered

What changes. What happens if you lose your phone. And what happens if you lose the codes too, which admits a wait of days and explains it: "That delay is deliberate. It is what stops someone else doing it."
