WHAT A MEMBERSHIP REALLY COSTS
The four prices
Monthly ratein the ad
Fee to joinin the agreement
Annual feein the agreement
Cost to leavein the agreement

Most signup pages show the first line. The other three surface in month three.

What this page shows instead
Todayfirst month + joining
Each month afterplan + add-ons
Once a yearwith its date
FIRST 12 MONTHSeverything included

One total you can compare between plans. Under it, the receipt says what the other plan would cost, even when the other plan is cheaper.

How leaving works

Printed directly above the pay button: how much notice, what it costs, whether you can pause. The annual fee is "first charged in your third month", so it arrives as a charge and not as a surprise.
