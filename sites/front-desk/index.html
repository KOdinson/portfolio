Front Desk How it worksTry itCase study

Pick a membership. See every dollar first.

Front Desk is a gym signup page that shows what you pay today, each month and once a year, the first-year total, and how leaving works. All of it before the pay button.

Try the prototypeHow it works

Live prototype with sample prices. Switch plans and add extras, and watch the receipt.

The problem

A membership has four prices. Most signup pages show one.

The headline number is the monthly rate. The other three live in the agreement and surface in month three.

Shown in the adMonthly rate
In the agreementFee to join
In the agreementAnnual fee
In the agreementCost to leave
How it works

A receipt that never leaves the screen.

Prices by when they hit

Three rows named for timing: today, each month after, once a year. That is how a person budgets.

One number to compare

The first-year total is the only highlighted figure. Two plans with different fees cannot be compared by monthly rate. Their totals can.

It argues against itself

Under the total, the receipt says what the other plan would cost, even when the other plan is cheaper.

Before you pay

Leaving is explained before joining.

"How leaving works" sits directly above the payment button: how much notice, what it costs, whether you can pause. And the annual fee comes with a date, "first charged in your third month", so it arrives as a charge and not as a surprise.
