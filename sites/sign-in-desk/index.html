Who is still in the building?

One tablet in the lobby, two people using it. Sign-In Desk is designed from both sides of the desk.

Try the prototypeBoth sides
The visitor's side

Thirty seconds, four taps and a name.

1Why you are hereThree large tiles: visiting, delivering, or working.
2Your nameThe only typing in the flow, alone on its screen.
3Who you are seeingPick the tenant. They are told you have arrived.
4One safety ruleFollow the green signs, do not use the lift, meet in the north car park.

The first screen says why: "It is how we know who to look for if the building has to be cleared."

The badge is the sign-out. Visitors hand it back on the way out, "so we know you are out". Anyone still listed at closing is someone to go and find.

One button turns the log into a headcount.

The same people, regrouped by floor, in large type with a tick box each. Built to be used outdoors, in a hurry, by someone holding a radio.

And it states its own boundary: "Tenants keep their own staff lists. This one is visitors only."
