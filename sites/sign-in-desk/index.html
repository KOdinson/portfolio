Who is still in the building?

Sign-In Desk is a lobby tablet for visitors and a live list for the guard. A visitor is through in thirty seconds. In an emergency, one button turns the same list into a floor-by-floor headcount.

Try the prototypeHow it works

Live prototype with an invented building. Sign in, switch to Guard view, then open the Emergency list.

For the visitor

Thirty seconds, four taps and a name.

  1. Why you are hereThree large tiles: visiting, delivering, or working.
  2. Your nameThe only typing in the flow, alone on its screen.
  3. Who you are seeingPick the tenant. They are told you have arrived.
  4. One safety ruleFollow the green signs, do not use the lift, meet in the north car park.
For the guard

One button turns the log into a headcount.

The same people, regrouped by floor, in large type with a tick box each. Built to be used outdoors, in a hurry, by someone holding a radio.

Design choices

A list is only useful if it is current.

The badge is the sign-out

Visitors hand the badge back on the way out, "so we know you are out".

Say why, on the first screen

"It is how we know who to look for if the building has to be cleared."

State what the list does not cover

"Tenants keep their own staff lists. This one is visitors only." A headcount that looks complete and is not would be dangerous.
