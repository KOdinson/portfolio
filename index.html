How I work

Start from the failure

Security work taught me to ask how a thing breaks before asking how it looks. I list the ways a screen can be misread, then design against the expensive ones first.

Prototype in the real material

I build interactive prototypes in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with AI pair-programming to move faster. Moving data exposes problems a static mockup hides, like labels that collide or a list that reorders under your cursor.

Say what isn't tested

Every case study here separates what I observed from what I assumed, and ends with the study I would run next. A design I can't yet defend with evidence is labelled as a concept.
